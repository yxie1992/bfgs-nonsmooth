# Narrative and article selection

The governing contrast is between controlled local polyhedral geometry and
regularity within a finite collection of smooth branch formulas. The article
studies unmodified full BFGS with Armijo–weak-Wolfe at differentiable accepted
points. Positive statements concern every infinite admissible run in a common
objective-level basin; negative statements exhibit one fixed deterministic rule,
one fixed objective and arbitrarily close failing starts. These quantifiers are
not interchangeable.

The article's proof-complete core is the actual principal-minor hierarchy, its
normalized scalar bridge, qualitative reciprocal-average growth, the comparison
certificate, radial/inward equivalence, centered smooth-plus-sublinear geometry,
and finite polyhedral partitions. The upper-regularity deterministic local
counterexample is central. The lower-curvature construction has a separate proof
and regularity regime; it will be included in the article only with its complete
persistence and embedding argument. Both remain in the companion.

The full weaker-assumption growth family, finite-horizon estimates,
superquadratic sums, all noncompact homogeneous clauses, metric-fiber lifting,
scalar certificate nonnecessity, planar/fixed-normal/parabolic extensions and
additional examples remain in the companion. A positive result does not disappear
because another has a similar conclusion under different assumptions. No
mathematical result is discarded in this reorganization. The original inventory
is synthesis; its formal exports are mapped to the operative substantive proofs.

The preferred 30–35-page article budget is editorial. Complete proofs and normal
journal typography govern the final selection. The planar and fixed-normal
results will be precisely identified as companion results if their full arguments
cannot be included. They will not be advertised as independently proved article
headlines. A documented length exception is preferable to a hidden proof gap.

The manuscript is for author review. Human identities, affiliations, consent,
funding, conflicts, submission decisions and archival identifiers are not
invented. Original reports and all closed formal/audit evidence remain unchanged.
