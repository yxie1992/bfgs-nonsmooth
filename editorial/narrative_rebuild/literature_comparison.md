# Primary-literature comparison for the revised opening

Fresh primary sources were reread on 12 September 2026. Full assumptions, exact version locators and access limits are recorded in `literature_primary_review.json`; the current theorem/formal-contract links are in `literature_comparison.json`. No third-party paper was downloaded into this repository.

| Prior source | What it establishes or asks | What the article adds, and the retained boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Lewis–Overton, Challenge 7.1](https://cs.nyu.edu/overton/papers/pdffiles/nsoquasi.pdf), printed p.160 | A probability-one package for semialgebraic locally Lipschitz objectives with bounded sublevels: execution, stationary cluster points, all-trial-value rates, and conditional spectral identification. | Deterministic convex successes and failures. Exceptional starts do not imply positive-probability failure; the accumulating local constructions are not finite semialgebraic descriptions. Item1 also excludes successful smooth-stationary termination, unlike our convention. |
| [Guo–Lewis, Theorems3.2,4.1,4.3](https://people.orie.cornell.edu/aslewis/publications/18-nonsmooth.pdf) | Smooth convex replacements extend Powell’s convergence argument under explicit open-region or positive-band conditions. | A direct full-BFGS matrix/energy route for the stated objective geometries. The predecessor is a substantial framework, not only one norm example. |
| Guo–Lewis, Corollary4.2 | Every Euclidean-norm BFGS sequence converges to zero in arbitrary dimension. | A known case inside the coercive homogeneous theorem; no novelty claim for norm convergence. |
| Guo–Lewis, Corollary4.4 and following paragraph | A subsequence for u² + abs(v) approaches a point on v=0; full convergence to zero is explicitly left open. | The named mixed-benchmark corollary proves whole-sequence convergence to(0,0), from the centered family and the same infinite admissible-run conventions. |
| [Xie–Wächter, v1 TheoremIV.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.08571v1) | Finite exposure of an unbounded descent direction for the two-piece unbounded affine model, with differentiable iterates. | Coercive homogeneous families are broader, but infinite-run value alternatives do not replace finite escape. Noncompact conclusions remain companion results. |
| [Gebken, published Theorem3.1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10957-026-03008-z), A2 and all B1 clauses | Local criticality for continuous smooth selections, conditional on an iterate limit, repeated activity and the stated matrix behavior. Its introduction still identifies the mixed benchmark as unresolved. | The stated convex-family theorems derive convergence from objective conditions. Gebken’s nonconvex/general quasi-Newton scope is not claimed as subsumed. |

The matrix ancestry comes from the existing foundation packet: classical Powell convergence, Byrd–Nocedal matrix analysis and Nocedal’s Section5 exposition. This rebuild does not claim a new full reading of the original Powell or Byrd–Nocedal proof. The specific current addition is the actual adjacent-degree principal-minor hierarchy and its reciprocal/comparison-energy consequences.
