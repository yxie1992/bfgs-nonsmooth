# Scientific positioning and proof selection

The governing question is what ordinary full BFGS can guarantee globally or
locally on convex nonsmooth objectives without assuming the desired iterate or
matrix asymptotics. The article now has three coequal contributions: explicit
deterministic failures, substantial convergence families including a named
published benchmark, and an analysis of actual BFGS matrices that tolerates
metric degeneration. Formal verification supports these contributions.

The narrative begins with smooth theory and nonsmooth empirical success,
Lewis–Overton's precise challenge, the real progress of Guo–Lewis and
Xie–Wächter, and the remaining gap made concrete by the mixed benchmark.
Three contribution paragraphs then connect the question to the answers.
Precise negative statements move to their proof section. A global/local
outcome table follows the gradient-geometry explanation rather than leading
the article.

The positive proof set now includes centered smooth–sublinear convergence,
a named whole-sequence mixed-benchmark corollary, coercive homogeneous and
max-linear/norm/gauge consequences, local finite partitions and composite
penalties, and the planar affine-critical theorem with its nondegenerate
smooth-max consequence and explicit curved-interface example. The planar
construction and endpoint estimates are proved in an article appendix.
The radial equivalence and all actual-matrix foundations remain complete.
Noncompact homogeneous value alternatives, fixed-normal higher-dimensional
geometry, flat-contour parabolic cases, rates, lifting and scalar baseline
results remain explicitly companion results; no noncompact theorem is an
independently proved article headline.

The counterexamples keep their exact fixed-function, fixed-search, fixed-SPD
and arbitrarily-close-start quantifiers. Their global consequences and two
incomparable local regularity regimes are explained separately. Matched
space/objective/matrix scaling is extracted as a reviewed mathematical lemma;
all-query orbit transfer remains tied to the actual certified embeddings.

The prior 32-page article did not use the available positive theory to establish
its scientific significance. We therefore prioritize complete global and
nonradial arguments and their context over that edition's shorter selection.
Final page count will be measured in the unchanged normal journal typography;
any exception to the earlier 30–35-page editorial budget will be documented,
not hidden by smaller fonts or outsourced essential proofs.

The corrected 172-span source record remains immutable. The companion keeps
all strongest incomparable results and aligns its opening and reader routes
with the broadened article. New correspondence records identify moved and
added article statements, complete export unions and the exact theorem
hypotheses. Original audits and the initial author-review release remain
preserved as dated evidence, not silently relabeled as new runs.
