# Journal fit and template decision

Policy access date: 12 September 2026. This is an authoring decision and a limited policy review, not submission authorization or a prediction of acceptance.

**Preferred target: Mathematical Programming, regular article.** The combined matrix, geometric and counterexample argument fits its optimization scope. The 30–35-page objective is this project's editorial budget. The official instructions state no general regular-article page cap; concision and complete, readable proofs govern selection.

The [MP instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/10107/submission-guidelines), under “Manuscript length and online supplemental material,” exclude extended text, proofs and code from published Supplemental Material. Additional Material can assist referees but is neither reviewed nor published. “Title Page,” “References” and “Text” require a 150–250-word abstract, 4–6 keywords, MSC information, numeric citations, and a reference list restricted to cited published or accepted work. Unpublished work is identified in the text. The journal-specific LaTeX link overrides the generic Springer template recommendation. Overlap with earlier versions requires the specified title-footnote and submission disclosures.

**Template used:** official `svjour3` class, version 3.2 dated 2007/05/08, with `smallextended` option and `\smartqed`; the supplied template is dated 2010/09/16. The older dates are the actual files supplied by the current journal-specific link, not guessed current version numbers. `editorial/vendor/svjour3/PROVENANCE.json` records the download URL, archive hash, retained file hashes and original notices. The class and supporting files are unmodified. No SPDX or broad open-source licence was asserted where the supplied files provide none. Build with this directory on `TEXINPUTS`; retain the normal one-column layout and native title/abstract/keyword conventions.

**Proof and companion strategy.** Essential arguments and finite certificate inequalities belong in the article and its own appendices. Claims whose proofs remain only in the complete companion are described as companion results and excluded from article theorem headlines. The companion is an independent repository research report with formal sources and audit evidence, not a journal-published supplement. Final pagination is measured by the release build and recorded in the build/review evidence; no page count is inferred from source length. The completed build has a 32-page article and a 105-page companion (`editorial/reviews/render_manifest.json`). The article meets the project's 30–35-page budget; this is measured pagination, not an official MP maximum.

**SIOPT alternative.** Its [editorial policy](https://epubs.siam.org/journal/siopt/editorial-policy) fits nonsmooth optimization but gives a 25-page policy in SIAM format, with longer papers exceptional. It does not publish supplements and accepts review supplements only when requested. Its [author instructions](https://epubs.siam.org/journal/siopt/instructions-for-authors) call for a one-paragraph abstract of at most 250 words and inline figures. The [current SIAM author package](https://epubs.siam.org/journal-authors) supplies `siamart251216.cls`. An SIOPT version would require substantive selection or a justified length exception; changing the class would not establish compliance.

**Disclosure and decisions.** MP requires disclosure of substantive LLM use. [SIAM's AI policy](https://epubs.siam.org/artificial-intelligence), version 2.0 effective May 2026, likewise requires disclosure and human responsibility. The accurate activity record and unapproved draft disclosure are in `AI_AND_AUTHOR_ACTIONS.md`. Authors must decide names, affiliations, corresponding contact, funding/conflicts, overlap disclosures, final responsibility statements and any submission or archival deposit. These unknown facts are excluded from the factual manuscript. Neither the literature ledger nor a Lean audit certifies bibliographic priority or human approval.

The article bibliography is trimmed to the 20 published works actually cited by the recursive `paper/master.tex` input graph. It includes the newly rechecked 2023 Wachsmuth–Wachsmuth article. The broader ledger and companion also retain the verified 2025 Jin–Mokhtari NeurIPS publication. `paper/preprint_references.tex` defines text identifiers for five unpublished works and two earlier-version pointers; four unpublished works are used in the current article. The ledger preserves the broader 39-work inventory independently of article selection and records all 30 active point-of-use citation contexts.
