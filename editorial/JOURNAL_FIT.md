# Journal fit and template decision

Policy access date: 12 September 2026; the official MP instructions were reopened during the scientific-narrative revision on the same date. This is an authoring decision and a limited policy review, not submission authorization or a prediction of acceptance.

**Preferred target: Mathematical Programming, regular article.** The combined matrix, geometric and counterexample argument fits its optimization scope. The 30–35-page objective is this project's editorial budget. The official instructions state no general regular-article numeric page cap, but expressly subject very long papers to increased scrutiny and permit rejection for length disproportionate to contribution. Concision and complete, readable proofs govern selection; absence of a numeric cap is not permission for unnecessary length.

The [MP instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/10107/submission-guidelines), under “Manuscript length and online supplemental material,” exclude extended text, proofs and code from published Supplemental Material. Additional Material can assist referees but is neither reviewed nor published. “Title Page,” “References” and “Text” require a 150–250-word abstract, 4–6 keywords, MSC information, numeric citations, and a reference list restricted to cited published or accepted work. Unpublished work is identified in the text. The journal-specific LaTeX link overrides the generic Springer template recommendation. Overlap with earlier versions requires the specified title-footnote and submission disclosures.

**Template used:** official `svjour3` class, version 3.2 dated 2007/05/08, with `smallextended` option and `\smartqed`; the supplied template is dated 2010/09/16. The older dates are the actual files supplied by the current journal-specific link, not guessed current version numbers. `editorial/vendor/svjour3/PROVENANCE.json` records the download URL, archive hash, retained file hashes and original notices. The class and supporting files are unmodified. No SPDX or broad open-source licence was asserted where the supplied files provide none. Build with this directory on `TEXINPUTS`; retain the normal one-column layout and native title/abstract/keyword conventions.

**Proof and companion strategy.** Essential arguments and finite certificate inequalities belong in the article and its own appendices. Claims whose proofs remain only in the complete companion are described as companion results and excluded from article theorem headlines. The companion is an independent repository research report with formal sources and audit evidence, not a journal-published supplement. Final pagination is measured by the release build and recorded in the build/review evidence; no page count is inferred from source length. The earlier 32-page article and 105-page companion in the preceding render manifest are historical evidence and do not describe this substantive revision.

**Provisional exception to the project's length budget.** The initial narrative-rebuild compilation measured **48 article pages**, including appendices and references (`narrative_rebuild/paper_first_build.log`). The integrated final candidate measures **47 article pages and 107 companion pages** after reduction; the final render and all-page review are underway. The added space serves a materially broader proof-complete positive contribution: the named whole-sequence mixed benchmark, centered and coercive homogeneous families, localization and finite partitions, and a constructed planar field with an explicit nonradial basin. The field requires compact corner geometry, secant estimates and signed transport proofs; the two incomparable finite-branch negatives still require their actual search and embedding certificates. Removing these proofs would change the advertised scientific scope or make a principal claim depend on the companion. The present authoring decision provisionally accepts exceeding 35 pages for that unified argument, while reducing repetition and peripheral alternatives before the final release. It is neither journal permission nor a claim that 48 pages is an accepted final length. Normal class typography and readable figures are retained. The final candidate reduces the initial rebuild by one article page without changing normal typography or removing its principal proofs. Final render hashes and all-page findings will be recorded in the release evidence.

**SIOPT alternative.** Its [editorial policy](https://epubs.siam.org/journal/siopt/editorial-policy) fits nonsmooth optimization but gives a 25-page policy in SIAM format, with longer papers exceptional. It does not publish supplements and accepts review supplements only when requested. Its [author instructions](https://epubs.siam.org/journal/siopt/instructions-for-authors) call for a one-paragraph abstract of at most 250 words and inline figures. The [current SIAM author package](https://epubs.siam.org/journal-authors) supplies `siamart251216.cls`. An SIOPT version would require substantive selection or a justified length exception; changing the class would not establish compliance.

**Disclosure and decisions.** MP requires disclosure of substantive LLM use. [SIAM's AI policy](https://epubs.siam.org/artificial-intelligence), version 2.0 effective May 2026, likewise requires disclosure and human responsibility. The accurate activity record and unapproved draft disclosure are in `AI_AND_AUTHOR_ACTIONS.md`. Authors must decide names, affiliations, corresponding contact, funding/conflicts, overlap disclosures, final responsibility statements and any submission or archival deposit. These unknown facts are excluded from the factual manuscript. Neither the literature ledger nor a Lean audit certifies bibliographic priority or human approval.
