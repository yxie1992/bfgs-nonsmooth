\section*{Routes through the complete theory}
The article proves the centered and coercive homogeneous global results,
the local finite-partition theorem and a genuinely nonradial planar
application, alongside both independent local failure constructions. This
companion retains the full corrected record, including conclusions with
incomparable hypotheses beyond the article's selection. Shared matrix
foundations are proved once in Chapter~\ref{comp:M}.

Chapter~\ref{comp:I} is the scope and implication map. Chapters
\ref{comp:T} and \ref{comp:R} develop the comparison-energy and inward-model
mechanisms. For the global family questions, read the centered applications
in Chapters~\ref{comp:R} and \ref{comp:F}, followed by the noncompact
homogeneous theory in Chapter~\ref{comp:H}. For moving geometry, read the
affine-critical preparation in Chapter~\ref{comp:A} and the complete planar
proof in Chapter~\ref{comp:C}. Chapters~\ref{comp:Q} and \ref{comp:U} retain
the distinct flat-contour and fixed-normal extensions; neither is absorbed
by the planar strict-contour argument. Chapter~\ref{comp:G} tests the limits
of the certificate itself. Chapters~\ref{comp:D} and \ref{comp:L} keep the two
local negative regimes separate. Appendix~\ref{comp:seed_certificate} spells out the finite
rational certificate common to their seed construction. Chapter~\ref{comp:E}
and Appendix~\ref{comp:correspondence} distinguish mathematical theorems,
exact formal contracts, conditional results and execution evidence.

Original source snapshots, corrections and sealed audits are preserved.
Appendix~\ref{comp:correspondence} records source versions and complete
theorem-level correspondence. Definitions, assumptions, questions and
historical execution records are distinguished from proved conclusions.
