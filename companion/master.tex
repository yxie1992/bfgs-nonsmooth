\chapter*{How to read this companion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to read this companion}
The article selects a self-contained proof of local convergence under finite
polyhedral geometry and the two independent local failure constructions.
This companion retains the complete corrected mathematical record, including
results with incomparable hypotheses that do not fit that article's theorem
budget. It groups the analysis by mechanism: common algebra, objective
certificates, geometry, applications, failure constructions and evidence.
The shared matrix foundations are proved once in Chapter~\ref{comp:M}.

Chapter~\ref{comp:I} is the scope and implication map. Read Chapters
\ref{comp:T}--\ref{comp:G} for the basic comparison-energy mechanism, then
Chapters~\ref{comp:F}--\ref{comp:U} for the additional objective and geometric
classes. Chapters~\ref{comp:D} and \ref{comp:L} keep the two local negative
regimes separate. Appendix~\ref{comp:seed_certificate} spells out the finite
rational certificate common to their seed construction. Chapter~\ref{comp:E}
and Appendix~\ref{comp:correspondence} distinguish mathematical theorems,
exact formal contracts, conditional results and execution evidence.

The original source snapshots, prior corrections and sealed audit outputs have
not been overwritten. The operative baseline is Git commit
\texttt{00362c0249262d182866f14bac756a7c4edac95c}; precise paths and hashes
are recorded in \path{editorial/BASELINE.json}. Every one of the 172 source
spans is retained with its full export list in
\path{companion/SOURCE_CORRESPONDENCE.json}. Thematic reordering, renumbering,
relocation of bibliographic notes and the explicit replacement of an unavailable
program reference are documented transformations. Definitions, assumptions,
questions and historical execution records are classified separately from
proved conclusions.
