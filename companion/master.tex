\section*{Routes through the complete theory}
The article proves the centered and coercive homogeneous global results,
the local finite-partition theorem and a nonradial planar application,
alongside both independent local failure constructions. This companion
contains the complete theory, including results beyond the article's
selection. Shared matrix foundations are proved once in
Chapter~\ref{comp:M}.

Chapter~\ref{comp:I} is the scope and implication map. Chapters
\ref{comp:T} and \ref{comp:R} develop the comparison-energy and inward-model
mechanisms. For the global family questions, read the centered applications
in Chapters~\ref{comp:R} and \ref{comp:F}, followed by the noncompact
homogeneous theory in Chapter~\ref{comp:H}. For moving geometry, read the
affine-critical preparation in Chapter~\ref{comp:A} and the complete planar
proof in Chapter~\ref{comp:C}. Chapters~\ref{comp:Q} and \ref{comp:U} give
the flat-contour and fixed-normal extensions, which reach objectives outside
the planar strict-contour theorem. Chapter~\ref{comp:G} tests the limits
of the certificate itself. Chapters~\ref{comp:D} and \ref{comp:L} construct
the two local counterexamples. Appendix~\ref{comp:seed_certificate} spells out the finite
rational certificate common to their seed construction. Chapter~\ref{comp:E}
and Appendix~\ref{comp:correspondence} describe the formal verification and
its correspondence with the text.

Original source snapshots, corrections and sealed audits are preserved.
Appendix~\ref{comp:correspondence} records source versions and complete
theorem-level correspondence.
