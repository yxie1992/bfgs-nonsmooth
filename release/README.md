The author-review article has **49 pages**, including its essential proof appendices; the complete companion has **108 pages**. The seven-section article architecture and its positive and negative theorem selection are unchanged. Since the 13 September edition, every item of both documents has been classified against Lean, and what this Lean coverage, the Lean review before it and a line-by-line reading of the article's proofs found has been corrected in both documents (`TODO_MANUSCRIPT_REVIEW.md`): the manuscript corrections M1–M6 on 13 October and M7–M41 on 15 October 2026, and in the companion the wording points W1–W11 and W14–W47 on 15 October; W12 and W13 are left to the author. The PDFs are `BFGS_Journal_Article.pdf` and `BFGS_Complete_Companion.pdf`; both print "Author-review edition, 15 October 2026". LaTeX entry points are `paper/master.tex` and `companion/master.tex`. ## Reproduction and source identity

In an existing repository checkout (the correspondence and release-input checks need its Git history):

```bash
bash editorial/build.sh
python3 editorial/release_20261015/refresh_correspondence.py --check
python3 editorial/verify_historical_correspondence.py
python3 editorial/lean_coverage/check_coverage.py --strict
python3 editorial/verify_release_inputs.py
```

The second command checks the October correspondence revision; the third replays the unchanged 13 September correspondence check at its release commit `3e8b5c6` (that check rejects every statement change by design, so its result at later commits is only recorded) and writes its own run directory. `verify_release_inputs.py` checks the cached Lean bytes when `bfgs_lean_stage1/.lake` and `bfgs_lean_stage1/.toolchain` are both present and match the sealed September host cache (`cache_binding_04`), and authenticates the receipts alone when both are absent; a rebuilt cache fails this check by design.

The Lean package of this edition is built and audited with the commands of `bfgs_lean_article/README.md` (pinned Mathlib cache of the main library; never `lake update` there; lean4checker at tag v4.19.0):

```bash
cd bfgs_lean_article && lake build
python3 scripts/axiom_audit.py --jobs 6 --report /tmp/AXIOM_AUDIT.json
python3 scripts/kernel_replay.py --checker-repo /path/to/lean4checker --self-test --report /tmp/KERNEL_REPLAY.json
```

The source archive `BFGS_paper_companion_sources.zip` preserves the outer `BFGS-non-smooth/` directory and every committed relative path, including Lean sources, pinned dependencies, complete proof/source maps, actual audits and review records. It excludes dependency caches, compiled objects, untracked user files and temporary build debris. The Git history bundle supports immutable historical checks. After a fresh extraction, run `python3 _archive/restore_git_history.py` before any other command. `_archive/RELEASE_SNAPSHOT.json` identifies the immutable source commit; any older receipt inside that snapshot is explicitly historical. The archive's own hash and its later validation record are external, avoiding a circular hash or future-execution claim.

Python 3.11 or newer, TeX Live with latexmk/pdflatex, Poppler and Node.js (for the historical gate) are required; `editorial/render_review.py` also needs Pillow, and the Lean commands need elan, which installs Lean 4.19.0 from `lean-toolchain`. The supplied journal class retains its original notices. Figure generation is reproducible standard-library Python/TikZ. `paper/figures/check_trial_display.py` independently checks the displayed rational current/trial intervals; it is diagnostic evidence, not a premise imported into Lean.

## Proof and review scope

Every item of both documents is classified in the Lean coverage registers (`editorial/lean_coverage/`): 619 items of the article and 1,856 of the companion. A claim has a Lean theorem with the same statement (1,120 items), or is covered by the Lean theorem of the item that it restates or weakens, is part of, or proves; definitions have their Lean definitions; the 12 open questions are stated in Lean without proof; history, motivation and descriptions of records are classified as non-claims, each with a reason. No item is left as a discrepancy (`check_coverage.py --strict`). A proof is covered by the Lean proof of its result; steps a printed proof leaves out are not formalized separately, and the article's proofs were read line by line.

The main Lean library (`bfgs_lean_stage1`, 736 modules) and the four September supplements are unchanged, and their retained audits are authenticated as before. The new Lean code is the package `bfgs_lean_article`: it compiles the article supplements in place and adds new modules, among them the lower-curvature failure for every admissible pair, and 33 coverage modules; it declares no axiom. The axiom audit of 15 October 2026 checked 5,420 Lean names, every name that the claim ledger or the coverage registers cite and the package's principal declarations, and found only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; the kernel replay with lean4checker v4.19.0 re-checked every declaration of 783 project modules (`bfgs_lean_article/AXIOM_AUDIT.json` and `KERNEL_REPLAY.json`, of commit `2cfa27a`). The release-input gate binds both reports to the frozen sources: no Lean file, Lake configuration, audit script, claim ledger or coverage register changed after the audited commit, and the reports cover every name and module that their scripts collect from the sources.

The October correspondence revision (`editorial/release_20261015/`) compares the article, and the two companion files whose passages the root review maps, with the 13 September release commit `3e8b5c6`. It lists 77 changed units (6 named statements, 10 labeled displays, 3 tables and figures, 1 algorithm block, 28 proofs, 6 negative-review proof passages, 12 root passages, 5 deduction passages, 2 complete appendices, 4 macro definitions), each with its correction items and the Lean coverage register items that state it. Every other unit (named statements, labeled displays, mapped tables and figures, proofs and anchored passages, the line-search algorithm, the complete text of the two appendices that the 13 September revision preserved, and the macro definitions) equals the baseline after removing the whitespace that does not separate TeX tokens, without notation aliases: 25 of the 31 named statements and 45 of the 55 labeled displays are unchanged. Text outside these units (headings, prose outside the mapped passages, unmapped figures) is bound by file hashes only. Every row was checked by an independent reviewer agent against `git diff 3e8b5c6` and the registers. The 13 September check stays unchanged and passes at its release commit. All 172 companion source spans and 3,087 distinct original exports are preserved. Full mappings are in `editorial/CLAIM_LEDGER.json`, `companion/PROOF_CORRESPONDENCE.html` and `editorial/release_20261015/mapping_refresh.json`.
