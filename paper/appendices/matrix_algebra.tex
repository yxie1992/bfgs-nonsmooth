\section{Gram compression for the inverse-BFGS update}\label{art:matrix-algebra}

This appendix establishes the subspace determinant inequality underlying the
principal-minor hierarchy (Theorem~\ref{art:minor-hierarchy}).

\begin{lemma}[Subspace Gram compression]\label{art:gram-compression}
Under the assumptions of Theorem~\ref{art:minor-hierarchy}, let $Z\in\R^{n\times j}$
have orthonormal columns ($1\le j\le n$). Then
\[
 \det(Z^TH_+Z)\le
 \frac{\det\operatorname{Gram}_H(y,Z)}{\beta^2q}
       +\frac\alpha\beta\det(Z^THZ),
\]
and at $j=n$ the exact identity $\det H_+=(\alpha/\beta)\det H$ holds.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $v=Hg$, $\kappa=g^THy=-\beta q$, and $P=I-vy^T/\kappa$. Because
$y^Ts=\alpha\beta q$, the inverse-BFGS update~\eqref{art:update} decomposes as
\[
 H_+=\mathcal F+\frac{\alpha}{\beta q}vv^T,
 \qquad \mathcal F=PHP^T.
\]
Writing
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 A=Z^THZ\succ0,\quad a=Z^THg,\quad b=Z^THy,\quad r=y^THy,\\
 u=a^TA^{-1}a,\quad w=a^TA^{-1}b,\quad z=b^TA^{-1}b,
 \end{gathered}
\]
the projected term compresses to a rank-two perturbation of $A$:
\[
 Z^T\mathcal FZ
 =A-[a\ b]
 \begin{pmatrix}-r/\kappa^2&1/\kappa\\1/\kappa&0\end{pmatrix}
 [a\ b]^T.
\]
Applying Sylvester's identity $\det(I+UV)=\det(I+VU)$ reduces the determinant
ratio $\det(Z^T\mathcal FZ)/\det A$ to a $2\times2$ determinant whose expansion
gives
\begin{equation}\label{art:compression-determinant}
 \det(Z^T\mathcal FZ)
 =\frac{\det A}{\kappa^2}
       \bigl((\kappa-w)^2+u(r-z)\bigr).
\end{equation}
For $\operatorname{Gram}_H(y,Z)=[y\ Z]^TH[y\ Z]$ and
$\operatorname{Gram}_H(g,y,Z)=[g\ y\ Z]^TH[g\ y\ Z]$, taking Schur complements
with respect to $A\succ0$ yields
\begin{align*}
 \det\operatorname{Gram}_H(y,Z)&=(r-z)\det A\ge0,\\
 \det\operatorname{Gram}_H(g,y,Z)
 &=\bigl((q-u)(r-z)-(\kappa-w)^2\bigr)\det A\ge0,
\end{align*}
both nonnegative as Gram determinants (even if their columns are linearly
dependent). Substituting these two identities into~\eqref{art:compression-determinant}
and using $\kappa^2=\beta^2q^2$ gives
\[
 \det(Z^T\mathcal FZ)
 =\frac{q\det\operatorname{Gram}_H(y,Z)
             -\det\operatorname{Gram}_H(g,y,Z)}{\beta^2q^2}
 \le\frac{\det\operatorname{Gram}_H(y,Z)}{\beta^2q}.
\]

Next, adding $\rho aa^T$ to $Z^T\mathcal FZ$ replaces the top-left entry
$-r/\kappa^2$ of the $2\times2$ coupling matrix by $-r/\kappa^2-\rho$, which by
the same $2\times2$ determinant expansion increases the determinant by
$\rho u\det A$ (without requiring $Z^T\mathcal FZ$ to be invertible). Since
$Z^Tv=a$, setting $\rho=\alpha/(\beta q)$ recovers $Z^TH_+Z$; moreover, the
Schur complement of $A$ in $\operatorname{Gram}_H(g,Z)\succeq0$ gives $0\le u\le q$,
so $\rho u\det A\le(\alpha/\beta)\det A$, proving the inequality.

Finally, when $j=n$, $Z$ is orthogonal, so $A^{-1}=Z^TH^{-1}Z$ gives $u=q$,
$w=\kappa$, and $z=r$: the right-hand side of~\eqref{art:compression-determinant}
vanishes, the rank-one increment equals $(\alpha/\beta)\det A=(\alpha/\beta)\det H$,
and orthogonal invariance gives $\det H_+=(\alpha/\beta)\det H$.\qed
\end{proof}
