## 1. Actual normalized algebra and rational regularity

Suggested module: `LNormalizedReturn`. Use five literal coordinates in `Vec 5`: (z0,z1,b,e,tau), with the matrix [[1,b],[b,e]]. Define an internal pair (Z,M), and perform exactly three updates at the fixed input tau, using the source's cyclic gradients and fixed lengths 1,1/4,1/16. In each substep define gamma=g_j+mu*tau*Z, S=-alpha*M*gamma, Y=g_next-g_j+mu*tau*S, and M_next=inverseBFGS M S Y. The return divides the final point and the (0,1)/(1,1) matrix entries by r=M_final(0,0), and multiplies tau by r. Do not renormalize between substeps and do not update tau during them.

First prove at Pstar=(z_seed,0,1,0) that every internal point, matrix, step, and secant is exactly the existing seed object, the three curvatures are positive, r=rho>0, and the actual return fixes Pstar. Existing `AlgebraicSeedCycle.source_cycle`, finite-cycle/geometry modules, and actual matrix update definitions provide the algebra. Keep a single named finite list of denominator functions (three curvatures and final r).

Next prove those denominators remain nonzero on a real open neighborhood of Pstar, and prove the actual rational map is analytic there. The neighborhood includes artificial negative tau. It is an analytic extension; physical BFGS correspondence is asserted only for positive tau. Pinned mathlib provides `AnalyticAt.inv`, `AnalyticAt.div`, and calculus/finite-sum composition in `Analysis/Analytic/Constructions.lean`. A proved C1 neighborhood already supports subsequent contraction work, but source analyticity should be retained as its own export rather than silently dropped.

## 2. Actual positive-scale BFGS and all-trial neighborhood agreement

Suggested module: `LNormalizedOracle`. Reuse `LQuadraticSeed.branch_hasGradient` and prove that positive scaling preserves the strict maximizing slope. The existing `inverseBFGS_scale` gives inverseBFGS(tau*M,tau*S,Y)=tau*inverseBFGS(M,S,Y) for tau nonzero; `bfgsStep_scale_matrix` handles the step. These are coordinate identities only; the stored physical matrix is never reset or rescaled by an algorithmic operation.

Construct all nine normalized query maps from their actual phase direction and listed trial length, with subsequent accepted states generated by the preceding map. Prove continuity of the finitely many branch-gap, rejected-value-gap, accepted Armijo, and accepted Wolfe expressions, plus current descent and positive curvature. Intersect the finite open neighborhoods to preserve every strict decision; then invoke `DeterministicLineSearch.search_accept` / `search_armijo_reject` to prove the literal three search traces and no unseen earlier acceptance. This closes agreement for every positive-tau state in the neighborhood, not just a chosen future orbit. SPD of the input shape follows from closeness to I; actual substep SPD follows from positive curvature and the real update theorem.

## 3. Differentiate the actual shape map before enclosing it

Use the exact root box and `IntervalBounds` to prove the four signs of lambda^2-tr(J)*lambda+det(J) at 38/1000,39/1000,1564/1000,1565/1000. The five-dimensional derivative must be derived from the real return map. Its diagonal blocks are rho^(-1) I2, J, and rho; lower-left blocks vanish. The source's global result needs each fixed mu>0, without any uniform neighborhood in mu.

## 4. Linear splitting and transversality

Suggested module: `LReturnHyperbolicity`. Prove the actual characteristic polynomial factorization and the eigenvalues with multiplicities: rho^(-1) twice, lambda_s, lambda_u, rho. Exact intervals separate all distinct values from zero and the unit circle. The repeated rho^(-1) block is scalar on the first two coordinates; a basis argument must still justify diagonalization if it is used in the nonlinear step.

Use the invertibility of the upper-left 4x4 block minus rho I to solve for an actual rho eigenvector with last coordinate one. This is stronger than merely knowing a stable eigenvalue exists: it supplies the positive physical-scale direction. With explicit eigenvectors one can construct a continuous linear coordinate equivalence and an adapted norm having stable norm <1/10 and unstable inverse norm <2/3 (the stated source intervals allow these choices). Prove the constants in the chosen norm; the standard coordinate operator norm need not satisfy them for a nonnormal derivative.

## 5. A minimal stable-orbit theorem sufficient for global failure

Suggested independent module: `LocalStableOrbit`. This can run alongside the finite rational derivative/certificate work. Prove a Lyapunov-Perron contraction for a local C1 map T(w)=A*w+N(w), with stable/unstable coordinate splitting, N(0)=0, DN(0)=0, and suitably small Lipschitz remainder on a small ball. Work on a complete closed ball of weighted bounded sequences with sup norm of eta^(-n)*norm(w_n), where max stable eigenvalue < eta < 1. `ContractingWith.exists_fixedPoint'` in `Topology/MetricSpace/Contracting.lean` and completeness of bounded maps in `Topology/ContinuousMap/Bounded/Basic.lean` are available building blocks.

For prescribed stable initial coordinate a, the fixed-point operator has the literal components

- stable_n = A_s^n*a + sum_(k<n) A_s^(n-1-k)*N_s(w_k),
- unstable_n = -sum_(k>=n) A_u^(-(k-n+1))*N_u(w_k).

Prove convergence of the backward series, weighted bounds, invariance of the sequence ball, contraction, exact recurrence, and geometric convergence of the fixed sequence. Local remainders suffice if the sequence ball stays in the map's domain; a global cutoff need not be assumed. To obtain positive tau, prove the initial graph differs from the chosen stable initial vector by a small-o error, then select a small positive multiple of the rho eigenvector with tau coordinate one. Positive tau persists from tau_new=tau*r and r>0 in the agreement neighborhood.

This minimal theorem proves the orbit existence required for L's global failure conclusion. It does not by itself formalize the source's additional description of a smooth two-dimensional stable manifold. If the smaller route is used, record that literal manifold regularity assertion separately until a graph differentiability/manifold theorem is proved.
