# Global quadratic scalar projection

This five-file, 33-declaration scope proves a genuine global nonlinear projection fragment: one quadratic polynomial equality, strict positivity test, or weak positivity test, with arbitrary polynomial coefficients in all retained parameters. All scalar and parameter values are covered, including identically zero, constant and linear specializations. The four final contracts are infrastructure results toward Module A; arbitrary Boolean conjunctions of several quadratic atoms and general arbitrary-degree projection remain open.

`RealQuadraticElimination` proves exact quantified equivalences. A real zero exists exactly when the quadratic coefficient vanishes and either the linear coefficient is nonzero or the constant vanishes, or when the quadratic coefficient is nonzero and the discriminant is nonnegative. Positive and nonnegative witnesses are similarly classified: a positive leading coefficient gives positive values at infinity; a zero leading coefficient reduces to the exact affine case; a negative leading coefficient is decided by strict or weak positivity of the discriminant. The completing-square identity and the actual vertex prove both directions in the concave case. The zero case uses the actual real square root and the checked quadratic formula. No discriminant oracle, root existence premise, leading-coefficient nonzero premise or fixed-degree specialization is supplied to the final scalar equivalences.

`SemialgebraicQuadraticProjection` applies those equivalences to the evaluations of actual multivariate coefficient polynomials. Every case is an explicit finite union/intersection/complement of polynomial equality or sign loci, including the actual discriminant polynomial. This constructs a quantifier-free Boolean witness for each existential projection. Complements give universal nonpositivity, strict negativity and nonvanishing conditions. Complementing a projected set is only used for the corresponding universal formula; it is not used to claim that existential projection commutes with arbitrary negation or conjunction.

`QuadraticCoordinateProjection` constructs the literal multivariate polynomial in the retained variables and a single additional coordinate. Evaluation is proved equal to the scalar quadratic formula. Its final result is semialgebraicity of the actual `coordinateProjection` of the literal zero, positive and nonnegative polynomial sets. The additional `Unit` coordinate is converted to a scalar by a proved exact equivalence, not by an assumed projection property.

`QuadraticDegreeProjection` removes reliance on a supplied three-coefficient presentation. For any actual polynomial in `Option (Fin n)` variables whose degree in the eliminated coordinate is at most two, the actual `optionEquivLeft` coefficient polynomial representation and finite polynomial evaluation formula give its three coefficients. The arbitrary-degree parameter coefficients are unrestricted. Their specialization may lose degree or vanish completely. The final projection theorem for this arbitrary actual input polynomial invokes the already proved coefficient-based elimination and has only the structural eliminated-coordinate degree bound as a hypothesis.

The exact targets are `RealQuadraticEliminationTarget`, `QuadraticPolynomialProjectionTarget`, `QuadraticUniversalConditionsTarget`, and `ActualDegreeTwoProjectionTarget`. They cover arbitrary finite parameter dimension, including zero. The predicates are the project's genuine finite Boolean polynomial semialgebraic syntax. This tranche supplies no general projection assumption and makes no source correction.

The remaining general theorem requires simultaneous sign realization for finite arbitrary-degree polynomial families. Existential witnesses for separate atoms need not coincide, so these single-atom equivalences do not verify projection of an arbitrary Boolean formula, even if every atom has degree at most two. Likewise this tranche does not prove finite semialgebraic path decomposition or the final affine-critical gap theorem.
