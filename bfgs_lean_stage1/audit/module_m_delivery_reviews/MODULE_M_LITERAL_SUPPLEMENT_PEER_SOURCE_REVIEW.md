## Actual degree n−1 potential and source horizon limit

`potentialPolynomial` is an actual finite polynomial with coefficients
`r^j * e(j+1)` for `j<n`. Its evaluation is proved equal to the existing
weighted potential. The degree bound is at most n−1, including the zero
polynomial in dimension zero. For nonnegative coefficients and t≥1, the actual
potential is bounded by its fixed value at r times `t^(n−1)`.

`sharpPrincipalPotential` uses the literal source choice
`eta = D_J / (c*(m−1))` for m≥2. The coefficient sequence is the actual
principal-determinant sequence of H_J; D_J is the actual summable tail of
predicted decreases. Positive D_J, positive dimension, PSD coefficients and a
strictly positive trace slot follow from the actual full-BFGS run. No unrelated
tail-budget parameter or matrix bound is assumed. The equality is with the
actual polynomial evaluated at m−1, and the finite bound is the fixed potential
at parameter D_J/c times `(m−1)^(n−1)`. Thus it gives the exact degree-n−1
polynomial growth in the source horizon, not merely some unspecified polynomial
bound.

The actual mth root tends to one by lower and upper positive real-power
bounds. J is fixed before the horizon limit; no uniform control of H_J as J
varies enters the proof. The final target requires only an actual FullBFGSRun,
c>0 and summability of its predicted decreases. Arbitrary real G is allowed
because only G² enters this algebraic statement, so the report's positive
gradient-bound setting is included without a new premise. Early horizons
where totalized division is used do not affect the eventual m≥2 limit.
