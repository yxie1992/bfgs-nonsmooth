# Positive pseudo-remainder specialization of the actual rational index

These two files connect the delivered arbitrary-degree symbolic pseudo-division to the independently proved actual rational-index recurrence. They do not introduce a query oracle or assume that parameter fibers are semialgebraic.

`PositivePseudoIndex` proves index invariance under multiplying the actual rational function by a strictly positive real constant, using both actual one-sided infinite-limit equivalences. An actual identity `C c*p=q*Q+r` with c>0 therefore preserves the index after replacing p by r, because the quotient polynomial is continuous. The zero-remainder and nonzero-remainder reciprocal branches are proved separately. It also derives the endpoint term as zero for even product degree and actual leading-coefficient sign for odd product degree, and proves its only possible values are −1,0,1.

`PositivePseudoIndexSpecialization` applies an actual ring homomorphism to the complete polynomial identity `(C lc(q))^(2*k)*p=q*Q+r`. A nonzero actual image of the leading coefficient proves the specialized divisor is nonzero by inspecting its original top coefficient. The even power is strictly positive regardless of the leading coefficient's sign. The theorem then gives the actual specialized index as zero for a zero specialized remainder, otherwise the endpoint term minus the reciprocal index. Actual degree decrease is supplied separately by the existing symbolic pseudo-division theorem; it is not invented or assumed into this recurrence.

The exact contract `PositivePseudoIndexSpecializationTarget` instantiates arbitrary multivariate polynomial coefficients and every real parameter point in every finite dimension.
