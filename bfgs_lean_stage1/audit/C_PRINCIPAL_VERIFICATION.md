## Mathematical chain checked

`PlanarConvexCorner` retains the source's locally convex C² branches, actual independent gradients, and the local inverse identity from the same actual branch-value map. Differentiating the defining curve at nearby levels and using that inverse identity proves common support pairing at every nearby tie point, including smooth tie points. It does not infer local jump cancellation from the base point alone.

`PlanarBranchGeometry` proves actual accepted gradients equal active branch gradients and obtains a genuine tie point by the intermediate value theorem. `PlanarPairControl` derives a uniform bound from continuity and the actual inverse of the two gradient rows. No inverse or matrix bound is assumed in the source-level theorem.

`PlanarCrossingSecant` splits the actual gradient difference into two Lipschitz remainders and the actual tie-point jump. The jump pairs to zero with the same comparison field at the tie point; field Lipschitz continuity gives a numerator of order step length. Actual gradient-row control and weak Wolfe give a step estimate with a quadratic remainder, which is absorbed for sufficiently small steps to yield curvature bounded below by a positive constant times step length. This uses no positive branch-Hessian lower bound. The exact correction is bounded by a constant times step length. Every norm conversion between Euclidean and finite-Pi norms has an explicit constant.

`PlanarCornerBounds` derives all local gradient, field, Lipschitz and cocoercivity constants from the actual convex C² chart and the comparison field. `PlanarLocalSecant` combines crossing and same-branch cases; `PlanarCompactSecant` makes the constants uniform with a finite buffered cover. No finite number of straight-segment intersections with a switching curve is assumed.

`PlanarSecantField` constructs **one** cutoff/velocity family that has the already verified transport geometry and these secant estimates. `PlanarSecantRun` derives the late actual correction bound and combines it with the late logarithmic transport theorem, actual support visibility, and Module M's full-BFGS scalar energy contradiction. Positive finite value limit, vanishing steps, bounded gradients and summable predicted decreases are all derived from the objective/run hypotheses. `PlanarConvergence` supplies the literal sublevel and local-basin wrappers.

No new assumptions on iterate, matrix, eigenspace, metric accuracy or finite path-length convergence occur. There are no custom mathematical axioms, placeholders, or unchecked computational proofs. The original report is preserved. The previously documented tangent-frame determinant orientation correction remains harmless for the absolute-value estimates used here.
