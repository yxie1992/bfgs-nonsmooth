This is a bounded review of the original counterexamples, their objective geometry, and their essential proof bridges. It is not a claim that unrelated chapters, historical software executions, or every approximate printed digit have been reverified by this audit.

## Full BFGS and deterministic search

`Definitions.lean` defines the displacement as `-α • H.mulVec g` and the matrix update as

`(I − (yᵀs)⁻¹ s yᵀ) H (I − (yᵀs)⁻¹ y sᵀ) + (yᵀs)⁻¹ s sᵀ`.

`ObjectiveDefinitions.AdmissibleBFGSRun` requires exactly those actual step/update equalities. It contains initial positive definiteness, positive lengths, actual accepted-point derivatives and ordinary Armijo/weak-Wolfe inequalities. It contains **no boundedness, summability, matrix-convergence, iterate-convergence, or accepted-orbit-existence axiom**. `ObjectiveRun.positive_invariants` derives positive definiteness at every later step and strictly positive actual secant curvature; denominators are therefore not justified by totalized division at zero.

`DeterministicLineSearch.search` was inspected case by case. It starts at length 1 with lower endpoint 0 and no upper endpoint; an Armijo failure replaces the upper endpoint, a Wolfe failure replaces the lower endpoint, a finite bracket is bisected, and an unbounded bracket doubles the lower endpoint. Its value is both the accepted length and the ordered list of **every attempted length**. `fromMetric` uses the actual coordinate derivative and the actual `-Hg` direction. `actual_coordinateGradient` proves equality with each supplied `HasFDerivAt` gradient.

The `5` in the final statements is a successful finite-termination bound. It is not a change that aborts a successful search or imposes the proposed lengths. `SearchFuel.lean` adds and kernel-checks `review20SearchFuelExtension`: any successful search has exactly the same result and history with arbitrarily more fuel. The prescribed length pattern 1, 1/4, 1/16 is consequently the output of the source scheduler. At most five trials are needed, with the three histories `[1]`, `[1,1/2,1/4]`, `[1,1/2,1/4,1/8,1/16]`.

Actual accepted and rejected queries are represented by the nine-element source trial list and its phase predicate. `trial_history_witness` covers each member of the actual returned history. D's final contract gives `HasFDerivAt` at every phase-compatible trial; the L global and local contracts give actual differentiability for every such trial. Thus none of these conclusions relies on querying a chosen subgradient at a kink.

For L, the specified implementation is the strict scheduler, exactly as in the source. `LNormalizedOracle.Conditions.physical_accept` derives both strict accepted inequalities from positive margins. `physical_reject` derives rejected Armijo decisions from a strictly negative margin, so its proof has no equality case. The exact non-strict final wrapper included in this audit is D's explicit source assertion; no separate newly formalized L non-strict wrapper is claimed here.

The new theorem `DEmbedding.review20ParameterIndependence` proves equality of those four entire functions between any two allowed parameter pairs, rather than inferring sameness from equal limits. The final target asserts identity initialization for every n≥1, full exact histories for every k and every allowed pair, and uniform locality before all future iterations and trials. Its finite exceptional index n=0 does not weaken the existence of a sequence approaching zero; the source construction uses n≥1.

## Stable-manifold hypotheses are discharged

The actual rational kernel certificate bounds the entries of the actual two-dimensional shape derivative, not an unrelated approximate matrix. `LShapeSpectrum.source_shape_roots` proves the two distinct real roots in (0.038,0.039) and (1.564,1.565). `LReturnOrbitCore.actual_block` and `LReturnSpectrum.actual_spectral_contract` identify the full actual five-dimensional derivative, its exact conjugacy and full characteristic polynomial, including the repeated ρ⁻¹ factor. The nonzero scale direction is derived in the actual stable coordinates.

`source_splitting` derives the stable contraction and inverse-unstable contraction norm bounds. `analytic_returnMap_seed`, `seed_fixed`, and `return_hasStrictFDerivAt` supply the actual analytic map, fixed point and derivative. The generic sequence construction is on a complete vanishing-at-infinity space; it derives orbits by contraction. The analytic lift and implicit-function construction produce a smooth graph on a genuine open neighborhood. `AnalyticStableSequence.exists_smooth_stable_sequence` identifies its bounded-sequence fixed point with the actual vanishing sequence through local uniqueness; it does not assert that every bounded sequence converges.

The final `lStableInvariantManifold` therefore has only μ and a neighborhood of the fixed point as inputs. It exports the smooth embedded rank-two graph, exact tangent equality to the actual derivative's stable set, arbitrarily small positive-scale points, actual forward orbits staying in the prescribed neighborhood and converging to the fixed point, and explicit local forward invariance of that same graph. `source_oracle_orbit` separately intersects with the strict-decision neighborhood and derives positivity of every later scale from the actual recurrence. There is no assumed forward-shooting accuracy, supplied stable orbit, or unproved invocation of an external stable-manifold theorem.

## Interpretation and scope qualifications

1. **Twice differentiable in smooth L.** The original says “classical Hessian” and “twice differentiable.” The actual smooth-L statement spells this out as first Fréchet differentiability on a neighborhood of x and differentiability of the derivative map at x. This is the usual iterated classical derivative setting. It deliberately does not treat differentiability of Lean's total `fderiv` alone at an isolated point as a classical second derivative. If “twice differentiable” were instead intended to mean only a Peano second-order expansion at one point, that distinct interpretation is not established by this target. The countable-polyhedral variant is stronger here: differentiability of F already implies existence of the second derivative and its fixed matrix value.
2. **Regularity distinctions.** D proves a maximum of four globally C² branches without a positive lower curvature bound. Global L proves three globally smooth strongly convex branches but its bad limit is not near its optimizer. Local smooth L proves four global C¹,¹ branches; local step L has countably many affine pieces. No theorem silently upgrades local L to four globally C² branches with a positive lower bound. The original itself leaves that stronger combined assertion open.
3. **Digits and provenance.** The existing exact precision proofs show all displayed D/L quantities are valid as loose approximations below one unit of the last displayed digit. Nearest rounding would change five D table entries and L's lower-left Jacobian display from `0.751501570427499` to `0.751501570427498`. These established display qualifications do not alter any strict margin, spectral interval, update identity or negative theorem. This audit does not treat unsupported historical execution claims as evidence and does not claim they were false.
4. **No broader negative claim.** Neither the source nor this audit proves positive-measure attraction, robustness of floating-point forward shooting, strong-Wolfe failure, or the excluded local four-C² uniformly strongly convex combination.
