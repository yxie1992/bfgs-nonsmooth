# Module R family coverage after the powered-gauge / finite-cell tranche

This map concerns source R's family and interpretation statements. It is not a replacement for the whole-project status register and does not label the entire report verified.

| Source claim | Exact delivered coverage / remaining scope |
|---|---|
| Signed finite sublinear gauge has zero inward error | `RadialPower.degree_one_inward_error_zero` proves the actual supporting error is zero at actual differentiable points; nonnegativity is not imposed in this p=1 statement. |
| Nonnegative gauge powers for all p≥1, including 1<p<2 | `PoweredGaugeRadial` proves actual homogeneity, convexity, actual objective radial pairing, scalar Taylor bound, mixed-sum IR and RS. `PoweredGaugeProfiles` proves the actual scalar first/second derivatives and a uniform actual second-derivative bound on every bounded set. |
| Arbitrary finite sums γ_j(A_j z)^p_j plus local C1,1 smooth term | `mixed_linear_powers_radial`, `AdmissibleBFGSRun.mixed_linear_powers_convergence`, and `local_mixed_linear_powers_basin`. Varying component dimensions and singular A_j are included; individual terms need not be coercive. Minima/compact basins remain exactly the source provisos. |
| Norm examples | Generic finite seminorms are explicitly instantiated. Concrete Euclidean, spectral and nuclear norm type/model identifications are not independently exported here. In particular no nonzero spectral residual is assumed smooth. |
| R:thm:plq finite polyhedral C1,1 extensions | `ConvexCellRadial.finite_polyhedral_basin` uses actual finite affine-inequality cells, proves their closedness/convexity, derives the neighborhood reduction, actual radial derivative equality, IR/RS and actual full-BFGS basin. Extensions have their own local open domains and need not be convex off cells. |
| Literal full-gradient equality at a differentiable point of an adjacent full-dimensional cell | Not needed by the main theorem's stronger radial proof. Separately compiler-clean in `audit/scratch/polyhedral_radial/CellDerivativeMatching.lean` using nonempty cell interior and derivative uniqueness. It is outside the frozen library/milestone until separately integrated/audited. |
| Finite convex piecewise linear-quadratic objective; convex smooth plus PLQ | `QuadraticCellRadial` derives local C1,1 regularity from actual quadratic coefficients and exports `piecewise_quadratic_basin` and `smooth_plus_piecewise_quadratic_basin`. No independent regularity assumptions on the quadratic pieces are used. Arbitrary max-quadratics remain excluded unless a valid polyhedral representation is provided. |
| Scalar affine residual penalties at nonzero optimizers: absolute value, hinge, quantile, fused/generalized l1 | The general max-affine/PLQ families cover actual finite polyhedral representations. Named residual-to-polyhedral representations and their local sign decomposition have not all been exported. Their positive local uniqueness conditions must not be replaced by arbitrary-start convergence. |
| Affine Euclidean group residuals, overlapping groups, sums of distances | Still require explicit local decomposition at zero/nonzero optimal residuals and derived local C1,1 regularity of the nonzero Euclidean norm residual. Abstract centered/mixed-gauge theorems do not by themselves verify this concrete decomposition. |
| Finite maximum of convex C2 branches with active radial derivative agreement | Open exact branch-selection/finite-selection Lipschitz argument, IR/basin consequences, and the converse genuine-tie radial kink obstruction. No finite-switching or eventual branch-identification hypothesis may be inserted. |
| Smooth-region radial Hessian and singular curvature caveat | Scalar real-power radial second derivatives are proved for that family. General radial-Hessian interpretation and a rigorous singular-jump counterexample to an a.e.-only test remain separate obligations. Existing central-difference/IR equivalence alone is not counted as those assertions. |
| Parabolic ridge boundary example | Still requires the actual objective minimizer, branch differentiability, descending pair, exact ratio `(u²−ε)²/(3ε)`, positive bands and every-neighborhood failure of RS. This is a certificate failure, not a bad BFGS trajectory. |
| Smooth plus nuclear-norm nonzero rank-deficient minimizer example | Still requires a symmetric-matrix/Frobenius model, actual nuclear norm and derivative/subgradient facts, strong convexity/optimizer, rotated rank-one spectral pair identities, ratio divergence and arbitrary-neighborhood positive-band failure of RS. No failure of BFGS is claimed by the source. |
| Intercept examples: degree one, u²+abs(v), degree p and D's four-piece values | The general intercept and Euler identities exist. This tranche does not add every named identity or D's exact branch-value cross-check. |

All original research sources remain unchanged. The finite-cell proof is stronger than the source theorem because full dimensionality and polyhedral structure are unnecessary for the radial conclusion; the explicit polyhedral wrapper still supplies the original source application from actual cells.
